## 1. Definitions

Terms such as *personal data*, *processing*, *controller*, *processor*, *data subject*, *personal data breach* and *supervisory authority* have the meanings given in the data protection laws that apply to the processing ("**Data Protection Law**"), which may include [the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the Saudi PDPL, the UAE PDPL, the Jordanian PDPL, or others].

"**Customer Personal Data**" means personal data that Aalim processes on the Customer's behalf in providing the service, as described in Annex 1.

"**Subprocessor**" means a third party that Aalim engages to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and scope

2.1 The Customer is the controller and Aalim is the processor of Customer Personal Data.

2.2 Aalim is a controller, not a processor, for its own account and billing data and for the personal development (Grow) data of individuals who use Aalim for themselves. This DPA does not cover that data; Aalim's privacy policy does.

2.3 The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, and the types of personal data and data subjects, are set out in Annex 1.

## 3. Customer's obligations

3.1 The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for the processing, for giving candidates and team members the notices Data Protection Law and employment law require, and for the lawfulness of its instructions.

3.2 The Customer will use the service in line with the Agreement's acceptable use and fair use rules, including that people, not the service, make decisions about candidates, and that Team mode is not used for dismissals, performance ratings or pay decisions.

## 4. Aalim's obligations

Aalim will:

4.1 process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are the Agreement, this DPA and the Customer's use and configuration of the service (for example its retention period), unless the law requires otherwise, in which case Aalim will tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it;

4.2 tell the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction breaks Data Protection Law;

4.3 ensure that people authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality;

4.4 implement the technical and organisational measures in Annex 2, and keep them appropriate to the risk;

4.5 not use Customer Personal Data to train AI models, and not sell it;

4.6 taking into account the nature of the processing, help the Customer respond to requests from data subjects (Aalim's product lets organisation admins delete a candidate's data, and lets people download or delete their own account data);

4.7 help the Customer with security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation, as far as these relate to Aalim's processing and the information available to Aalim;

4.8 make available the information needed to show compliance with this DPA, and allow audits as set out in section 8.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for Aalim to use the Subprocessors listed in Annex 3.

5.2 Aalim will give the Customer at least [30] days' notice before adding or replacing a Subprocessor, by [email to the Customer's admins / an update to the published list]. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period; if the parties can't resolve the objection, the Customer may terminate the affected part of the service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

5.3 Aalim will impose data protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective than this DPA, and remains responsible for its Subprocessors' performance.

## 6. Personal data breaches

6.1 Aalim will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

6.2 The notice will describe, as far as known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. Aalim will provide further information as it becomes available.

## 7. International transfers

7.1 Customer Personal Data is stored in [HOSTING_REGION]. Some Subprocessors may process it in other countries, as listed in Annex 3.

7.2 Where Data Protection Law requires a transfer mechanism, the parties agree to [the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 or 3, as applicable), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and/or the transfer mechanisms required by the Saudi, UAE or Jordanian PDPL], which are incorporated by reference, with the elections set out in [Annex 4 / an attached schedule].

## 8. Audits

8.1 Aalim will answer reasonable written questions about its processing and provide [summaries of independent assessments or security documentation] where available.

8.2 Where that is not enough to show compliance, or a supervisory authority requires it, the Customer may carry out an audit, at most [once a year], on [30] days' notice, during business hours, at its own cost, through an auditor bound by confidentiality, and without access to other customers' data.

## 9. Deletion and return

9.1 During the Agreement, Customer Personal Data is deleted according to the retention period the Customer sets in Aalim (12 months by default), and when the Customer deletes a candidate's data.

9.2 When the Agreement ends, the Customer may export its data (candidate lists and reports) while its account remains open. Aalim will then delete the remaining Customer Personal Data within [30] days, unless the law requires it to keep it, and confirm deletion on request. Data in backups is deleted as the backups are replaced, within [period].

## 10. Liability and precedence

10.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Agreement, except where Data Protection Law does not allow them.

10.2 If this DPA and the Agreement conflict, this DPA prevails as regards the processing of Customer Personal Data. If the transfer clauses in section 7 conflict with this DPA, the transfer clauses prevail.

## 11. Term

This DPA lasts as long as Aalim processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

These are the measures implemented in Aalim at the date of this DPA.

**Tenant isolation and access**
- Row-level security in the PostgreSQL database on every table that holds organisation or personal data, enforced through a separate, least-privilege runtime database role that cannot bypass the policies; the organisation and user for each request are set inside that request's own transaction, so pooled connections cannot leak context.
- Permission checks in the application as well as in the database; automated tests prove that one organisation cannot read another's data.
- Role-based access (recruiter, hiring manager, team manager, organisation admin); at least one admin per organisation.
- Equal-opportunity answers and AI request logs are unreadable by the runtime database role.

**Encryption and secrets**
- HTTPS for all traffic, with HSTS.
- Passwords stored only as salted hashes; candidate, invitation and report-share tokens stored only as SHA-256 hashes; share links expire and can be revoked.
- Webhook signing secrets encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM; webhook deliveries signed with HMAC-SHA256.
- Encryption of stored data and backups as provided by the database and hosting providers ([describe]).

**Application security**
- Strict Content Security Policy with no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers; framing by other sites blocked; other standard security headers.
- Sign-in rate limiting.
- Links that carry a token are excluded from search indexing, sent with no referrer and not cached.
- Webhooks refuse private, loopback and link-local addresses (checked at save time and after DNS resolution) and do not follow redirects.
- CSV exports neutralise formula injection.
- Error reports exclude request headers, cookies and tokens, and mask the secret part of private links.

**Accountability and oversight**
- An audit log of sensitive actions (viewing, sharing, exporting, adjusting and deleting reports; membership and settings changes; data exports and deletions), visible to organisation admins.
- Every AI request is logged (input, output, cost, timing), visible only to Aalim's platform team.
- Human oversight built into the product: no automatic rejection; reviewer adjustments require a written reason and are recorded; candidates can request that a person review every score, and the report cannot be shared or exported until that is done.
- Monthly AI spending caps per organisation, with an offline engine when they are reached.

**Data minimisation and lifecycle**
- The AI scorer receives only the scenario, its rubric and the answer: no names or other answers.
- Configurable retention with a nightly deletion sweep; per-candidate deletion by organisation admins; account export and deletion for individuals.
